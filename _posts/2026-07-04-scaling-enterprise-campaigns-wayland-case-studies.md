## Implementation

### American Express: Localizing Global Excellence

American Express tasked us with adapting their "Shop Small" initiative for the Spanish market — a campaign designed to promote local commerce and business coverage. The implementation required balancing global brand standards with local cultural resonance.

**Execution Steps:**
- **Audience Analysis:** Deployed Deep Data Analysis to understand Spanish cardholder behavior patterns and local commerce engagement drivers
- **Content Ecosystem:** Developed integrated content for apps and CRM systems, delivering personalized information, experiences, and offers tailored to different card types
- **Multi-Channel Activation:** Built social media presence and managed paid media impressions to drive awareness and participation
- **Localization Strategy:** Adapted global creative concepts to reflect Spanish cultural values and local business relationships

The campaign earned American Express's internal award for Best Adaptation of a Global Concept to a Local Market. This recognition validated our approach of creating ideas large enough to live in any format yet precise enough to generate results.

### IKEA: Crisis Communication and Employee Engagement

IKEA asked us to develop a campaign to activate new channels of information with employees following a series of global reputation crises. Many employees did not have direct access to company news, especially regarding controversial PR issues. The challenge required both technological infrastructure and emotional intelligence.

**Execution Steps:**
- **Concept Development:** Created the campaign concept "En IKEA no nos hacemos los suecos" to communicate corporate transparency and employee empowerment
- **Channel Innovation:** Established a WhatsApp communication channel to answer employee questions and send regular corporate updates
- **Content Production:** Filmed three audiovisual pieces with a friendly, humorous tone to humanize corporate communications
- **Incident Management:** Built the WhatsApp channel into a responsive system for handling internal incidents arising from shop staff inquiries

This implementation showcased our ability to design custom pathways for each brand's unique situation. The WhatsApp channel became a regular corporate update channel for all employees, transforming crisis management into ongoing engagement.

### FNAC: Driving Digital Traffic Through Strategic Content

For FNAC, we implemented strategic content initiatives that tripled web traffic. This demonstrated our ability to combine emotional targeting with data-driven optimization to achieve measurable digital performance outcomes.

### Telefónica: Cultural Sponsorship and Branded Content

For Telefónica, we developed a sponsored TV program at zero cost to the network, financed through branded content and cultural sponsorship actions for both the program and social media campaigns. The program applied the six degrees of separation concept to the world of art and culture, designed for art lovers who use social networks for entertainment and leisure.

This project showcased our ability to create innovative content formats that deliver brand value while providing genuine audience engagement.
