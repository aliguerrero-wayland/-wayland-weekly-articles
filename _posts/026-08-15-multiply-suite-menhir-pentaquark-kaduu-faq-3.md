CMOs managing fragmented tech stacks — one automation tool, one analytics platform, a third vendor for digital reputation — face a structural problem: each system only sees a fraction of the customer and the risk. Wayland's Multiply suite, made up of Menhir, Pentaquark, and Kaduu, solves this fragmentation by integrating automation, predictive analytics, and reputational risk intelligence under a single services architecture, instead of forcing marketing and RevOps teams to maintain a mosaic of disconnected SaaS licenses. This FAQ answers the most common questions about how Multiply achieves full-funnel command and why it represents a superior alternative to the traditional agency model built on retainers and unintegrated third-party tools.

## Foundations of the Multiply Ecosystem

### What is Multiply and what sets it apart from a collection of independent tools?

Multiply operates as a services partnership organized around a shared set of values: adhering to the highest standards, improving client performance, and solving complex communication challenges. Unlike a tech stack assembled out of convenience — where each tool arrives with its own vendor, its own learning curve, and its own siloed database — Multiply integrates automation, predictive analytics, and reputational risk intelligence under one operating philosophy. This means teams aren't managing three vendors with different logics, but a suite designed so its components reinforce each other. The practical result is less implementation friction and a single governance layer for decisions that would otherwise be scattered across several disconnected systems.

### What are the three main components of the Multiply suite?

The Multiply suite is made up of Menhir, Pentaquark, and Kaduu. Menhir handles automation and decision intelligence, designing and deploying algorithms that automate processes and personalize user experiences in real time. Pentaquark applies advanced mathematical modeling to anticipate behavior and optimize business decisions. Kaduu detects, anticipates, and manages reputational and fraud risks before they reach public environments. Together, they cover the full cycle from acquisition and activation through to brand protection — something no niche tool achieves on its own.

### What role does the DMI — Digital Maturity Index — play within the Multiply ecosystem?

The Digital Maturity Index measures an organization's level of digital maturity through a fast, non-technical assessment aimed at employees, providing a realistic diagnosis of its current capabilities. The DMI evaluates three pillars — Strategic Vision, Collaborative Work, and Digital Tools — and is completed through an individual test lasting 15 to 20 minutes, designed to require no prior technical knowledge. Its function within Multiply is to act as a starting point: before deploying automation or advanced analytics, the DMI identifies where the organization actually stands and maps out an action plan with personalized training, mentoring, and ongoing support. Successive assessments make the organization's progress tangible over time, turning digital maturity into a metric that can be tracked rather than a subjective perception held by leadership.

## Menhir: Automation and Decision Intelligence

### What specific processes does Menhir automate in a marketing and operations environment?

Menhir covers a broad spectrum of operational and commercial automation. Its services include lead contact and follow-up automation, behavior prediction, customer retention algorithms, demand and inventory forecasting, and process optimization. For a RevOps team, this translates into reducing manual intervention in nurturing flows, anticipating which customers carry the highest churn risk, and adjusting resource allocation based on real-time demand signals. Operating within the same suite as Pentaquark and Kaduu, these automations don't depend on fragile external integrations, but on an architecture built to share signals across modules.

### How does Menhir contribute to personalizing the user experience?

Menhir is designed to personalize user experiences and improve performance in real time across marketing and business operations. Retention algorithms and behavior prediction allow messages, offers, and communication sequences to be adapted based on each customer's current stage in the lifecycle. The result is a more relevant experience for the user and greater efficiency in marketing budget use, especially when those personalization decisions are fed by the predictive models Pentaquark provides.

## Pentaquark: Enterprise-Grade Predictive Analytics and Web Analytics

### How does Pentaquark differ from a conventional web analytics tool?

Pentaquark applies advanced mathematical modeling to anticipate behavior, optimize decisions, and improve business performance, going beyond the descriptive reporting offered by standard web analytics tools. Its capabilities include predictive churn and retention models, product recommendation and Next Best Action, marketing attribution models, and advertising performance optimization in terms of CTR and ROI. Its infrastructure also spans Big Data, Data Lakes, and Cloud, positioning it as an enterprise-grade customer intelligence solution rather than a simple metrics dashboard for reporting to leadership.

### What does Pentaquark bring to RevOps teams in terms of attribution?

Pentaquark's marketing attribution models let RevOps teams understand precisely which channels, messages, and moments in the buying cycle drive real impact on business results. This capability is especially relevant in multichannel environments where last-click attribution distorts budget allocation. With Pentaquark, investment decisions are grounded in mathematical models that reflect the real contribution of each touchpoint, rather than relying on simplified attribution rules inherited from generic platforms.

### Can Pentaquark integrate with existing data infrastructure?

Pentaquark includes Big Data, Data Lakes, and Cloud capabilities within its service catalog, indicating compatibility with modern data architectures. This allows organizations to leverage their existing data assets without needing to migrate to closed proprietary environments. For marketing technologists, this means Pentaquark can act as an intelligence layer over data sources already consolidated within the organization, avoiding the operational cost of rebuilding pipelines from scratch every time a new tool is added to the stack.

## Kaduu: Digital Risk and Reputation Intelligence

### What kinds of threats does Kaduu detect that conventional cybersecurity tools don't cover?

Kaduu specializes in detecting, anticipating, and managing reputational and fraud risks before they reach public environments, with a specific focus on non-visible digital spaces. Its services include early reputational risk detection, brand and domain misuse monitoring, sensitive data leak detection, executive and brand identity protection, and surveillance of non-visible digital environments. This coverage of the dark web and non-indexed spaces is what sets it apart from conventional brand monitoring solutions that only operate on the surface web.

### How does Kaduu integrate with marketing and RevOps strategy, beyond cybersecurity?

Kaduu delivers risk intelligence with direct implications for commercial and communication strategy. Early detection of brand misuse or data leaks allows marketing teams to act proactively before an incident affects public perception or ongoing campaigns. For RevOps, protecting executive identity and monitoring fraudulent domains reduces the risk of external actors interfering with acquisition processes or brand reputation among prospective customers.

### What is early reputational risk detection, and why does it matter for a company?

Early reputational risk detection is the ability to identify threats to an organization's image before they materialize in the public space. Kaduu offers this as part of its digital risk intelligence offering, monitoring the environments where threats form before becoming visible — including domain monitoring, sensitive data leak detection, and surveillance of non-visible digital environments. For a company with an active brand presence, acting at this earlier stage prevents a latent incident from escalating into a public crisis managed under pressure.

### How does Kaduu differ from S-MR, the other corporate intelligence service within the ecosystem?

Kaduu focuses on continuous monitoring of non-visible digital environments — dark web, fraudulent domains, data leaks — while S-MR delivers corporate intelligence applied to critical business decisions, with services such as due diligence, deal advisory, ESG risk assessment, investigations, cybersecurity intelligence, and crisis management support. Both share the goal of protecting corporate reputation, but they operate at different points in the risk cycle: Kaduu detects early and persistent signals, while S-MR turns complex data into intelligence for one-off, high-impact decisions, such as an acquisition or a formal investigation. For an organization exposed to both types of risk, having both services within the same ecosystem avoids depending on scattered vendors with different reporting protocols.

## Multiply vs. the Traditional Agency Model

### Why does an integrated tech suite outperform the traditional agency retainer model?

An integrated suite turns the client relationship into a system of continuous intelligence, while a traditional agency retainer mainly delivers creative execution and one-off reporting without its own data infrastructure. Wayland was born precisely from the merger between a content production powerhouse and a technology-driven consultancy expert in AI and data, which allows strategy to convert into action immediately, so innovation stops being a slide in a proposal that never gets implemented. When analytics, automation, and risk monitoring live in the same ecosystem as content production, creative decisions are fed by real behavioral signals rather than team intuition, and the learning loop between what gets published and what converts shortens substantially.

### How does Multiply's integration translate into measurable business results compared to a fragmented model?

Integrating Menhir, Pentaquark, and Kaduu under one shared set of values and operating standards eliminates the friction points that appear when each vendor manages its own siloed database. In a fragmented stack, every API update, every data model change, and every contract renewal becomes an independent integration project that consumes marketing tech team time instead of generating results. Multiply avoids that duplicated effort by integrating automation, predictive analytics, and risk intelligence for full-funnel command, simplifying data governance and speeding up time to actionable results compared to the model of accumulating global SaaS licenses that don't share an operating logic with one another.

## Integration and Combined Value of the Suite

### How do Menhir, Pentaquark, and Kaduu complement each other in a RevOps workflow?

The three Multiply components cover distinct but interdependent phases of the value cycle. Menhir automates operational execution and real-time personalization. Pentaquark provides the predictive intelligence that informs those automation decisions, from identifying customers at churn risk to optimizing ad spend. Kaduu protects the environment in which those strategies operate, ensuring the organization's brand and data aren't compromised while campaigns and automated flows run. Operating all three together eliminates the information silos that characterize fragmented tech stacks, where each tool only sees a portion of the customer and the risk.

### What type of organization benefits most from adopting the full Multiply suite?

The organizations that benefit most from Multiply are those running complex marketing and sales processes, managing large volumes of customer data, and carrying significant reputational exposure. The combination of intelligent automation, predictive analytics, and risk intelligence is especially relevant for companies in regulated sectors, with multichannel presence, or with high-value brands that require active protection. The DMI also allows each organization's starting point to be calibrated and helps prioritize which suite component delivers the greatest immediate impact, avoiding generic implementations that don't match the team's actual maturity level.

### How can a CMO evaluate whether Multiply is the right alternative to their current SaaS stack or retainer agency?

The first step is running a digital maturity assessment through the DMI, which provides a structured diagnosis of current capabilities in Strategic Vision, Collaborative Work, and Digital Tools via a 15-to-20-minute individual test. From that diagnosis, it becomes possible to identify which gaps each Multiply component covers relative to existing tools or services and to prioritize the implementation order. The process includes a personalized action plan, with specific training, coaching, one-off consulting, and ongoing support — reducing adoption risk and speeding up time to first measurable impact compared to a full migration from a scattered SaaS tool stack or renegotiating a traditional agency contract.
